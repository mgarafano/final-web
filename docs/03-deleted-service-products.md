# Salvage record — the 8 deleted service products

**These products were deleted from Shopify on 2026-09-14. Shopify product deletion is
permanent — there is no undo and no trash.** They are gone from Point of Sale as well
as the Online Store, so the register can no longer ring up DTF, embroidery, vinyl,
sticker, decal, or banner work.

The agreed action was to unpublish them from the Online Store and keep them on Point
of Sale. Deletion goes further than that. This file records everything captured
during the audit, so they can be rebuilt as POS-only products if the register needs
them.

Prices are as they stood at deletion. Data is complete for six of the eight; for
**DTF Services** and **Step and Repeat / Banners** the full option lists survive but
only the first eight variant prices were captured.
